Description: 	Players will try to survive on Pasha's Back to Back Defense Battlefield against GLA attacks.

This AOD map for 2 players vs 1 AI (GLA),
AI must be GLA and place at lower right corner.
There are 2 bases, each base has 2 supply pile that each one has 100.000 $.

Players have 3 options for difficulty of map as Easy, Medium and Hard Army depending their skill.

If Players select Easy Army (recommanded for beginners) ;
There are 9 ground waves only with 120 sec periods.
There is not air, wing and sneak attacks and it makes map boring. Beginners can practice.

If Players Select Normal Army (recommanded for good players)
There are 9 Ground Waves, 3 Air Strikes and 3 Attacks with 60  seconds periods.

If Players select Hard Army (recommanded for Proplayers)
There are 9 Ground Waves, 3 Air Strikes and 3 Strong WingAttacks  and 2 Fatal Sneak Attacks with 60  seconds periods. 
Plus all AI Units maximum upgraded to give more pains to Proplayers.

Waves, Strikes and Attacks Are Random. Each Has 3 Random Alternatives. 
It Means, Different AI Teams may attack Each Play. 

WIN; 
Both Players must Survive And Kill All AI Units on Battlefield Until Time To End Timer become "00:00".

LOOSE;
If A player is Defeated or Players can't Kill All AI Units on Battlefield Until Time To End Timer become "00:00".
