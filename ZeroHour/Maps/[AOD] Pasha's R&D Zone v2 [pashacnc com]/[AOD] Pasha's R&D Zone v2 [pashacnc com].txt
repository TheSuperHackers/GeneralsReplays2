A 3 players AOD map,

Players have 3 options for difficulty of map as Easy, Medium and Hard Army depending on how skilled they are
Easy Army: Waves periot 45 seconds
Medium Army: Waves periot 30 seconds
Hard Army:  Waves periot 30 seconds and Laser Turret, Patriot Battary, EMPs and also Auroras are not buildable for USA Armies. 
It makes map for Proplayers ONLY as all Pasha's Map.

AI must be GLA and place at upper right corner.

There are 3 bases and each base has 2 small supply piles that has 90.000 $ each and 25K Dollar crate.
Players can capture EMPs against air attacks of AI to R&D Zone buildings. Players can fill the reinforced bunkers
Plenty of money and goodies to capture against AI.

AI waves are attacking from ramps at south. Quick defense required for first rush attack.
Sneak attacks will come from different points on battlefield.

Win; 
Player win if the players can defend Pasha's Research and Development (R&D) Zone against AI attacks and kill all AI units on battlefield
until Victorious Time become "00:00"

Loose;
Players loose if Secret Research Lab, Secret Lab, Baikonur Ground Control Center and Infa China Power Plant buildings 
in Pasha's R&D Zone are destroyed by AI units
or if Players can't kill all AI unit on battlefield until Victorious Time become "00:00"
